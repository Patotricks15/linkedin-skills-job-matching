\section{Feature Engineering}
The declarative catalogue in \texttt{conf/features.yaml} covers the
candidate profile (skill count, seniority, taxonomy breadth), the candidate
job (category popularity, trailing posting volume), the profile-job
interaction (the stage-1 BM25 score itself, raw and taxonomy-normalized
skill overlap, seniority match), and context (location match, a
point-in-time posting-recency gap). Every aggregate over training postings
is filtered with a strict $t<\text{as\_of}$ comparison, never $\le$: an
event exactly at the cut-off belongs to the future partition. Schema,
leakage, and offline/online parity tests enforce this contract, including
a dedicated control that forges a future query and asserts the fitted
item-level features do not move. The generated feature catalogue, with its
